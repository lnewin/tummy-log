How this is worked out

For every time you ate a food, the app checks whether you logged a symptom inside the time window above. Hard poops (type 1 to 2) and loose poops (type 6 to 7) count as symptoms too. That gives the food's symptom rate, which is compared with your usual rate across all meals (the dark tick on each bar). Foods eaten only a few times are pulled towards your usual rate, so one bad day can't top the list. "Strong" needs at least 5 meals, 3 of them followed by symptoms, a rate of 50% or more and 1.3 times your usual rate. Foods often eaten together (like bread and butter) can share the blame, so try cutting one at a time.

Context and symptom days

Share of days with any symptom, comparing days with and without each factor. Only days where you saved a day context count.

These patterns are clues, not a diagnosis. If you have ongoing or severe symptoms, weight loss, blood in your stool or symptoms that wake you at night, speak to a GP. A food diary like this one is useful to bring to that appointment.

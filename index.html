How this is worked out

For every time you ate a food, the app checks whether you logged a symptom inside the time window above. That gives the food's symptom rate. It then compares that with your usual rate across all meals (the dark tick on each bar). A food needs to be eaten at least 3 times before it gets a verdict. "Strong link" means symptoms followed at least half the time and at least 1.3 times as often as usual. Foods often eaten together (like bread and butter) can share the blame, so try cutting one at a time.

These patterns are clues, not a diagnosis. If you have ongoing or severe symptoms, weight loss, blood in your stool or symptoms that wake you at night, speak to a GP. A food diary like this one is useful to bring to that appointment.
